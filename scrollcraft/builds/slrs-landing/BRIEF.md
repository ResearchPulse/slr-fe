# SLRS landing page brief

## Audience and product
SLRS is a systematic literature review workspace for researchers, graduate students, and review teams. It supports protocol setup, literature identification, study selection, extraction, quality assessment, synthesis, and reporting in a collaborative, traceable workflow.

## User direction
- Preserve the SLRS logo, existing typography, white-dominant canvas, and blue accent.
- Keep the existing shared header, footer, routes, authentication, and CTA behavior.
- Keep text concise and use the current product UI and HTML/CSS/SVG previews only. Do not generate imagery or video.
- User feeling curve: "Điềm tĩnh → khám phá → tin tưởng" (calm → discovery → trust).
- User wants distinct scenes with clear transitions: "Các cảnh riêng biệt, có nhịp chuyển rõ".
- Peak idea: "quy trình bài báo rời rác -> thành một quy trình rõ ràng liên kết".
- Signature interaction: "các nghiên cứu rời rạc dần kết nối thành dòng bằng chứng".
- Assets: "chỉ dùng tài sản hiện có".
- Primary action: Start a Review. Secondary action: View Projects.

## Page grammar and constraints
Custom grammar: Research Evidence Sequence. Keep the real app navigation and product cues, move through distinct scenes, and pin only the evidence-connection peak. No continuous worldflight, full-page dark or cream colors, generated images, invented metrics, fake controls, identical card grids, or repeated pinned scenes.

## Emotional journey
1. Calm: introduce the research record and review workspace.
2. Discovery: reveal protocol, identification, screening, extraction, and appraisal stages.
3. Clarity: show independent decisions and structured evidence in context.
4. Trust: connect each decision and finding to its source; show reporting support accurately.
5. Resolve: finish with a direct action to start a review or view projects.

## Scroll score
- Hero: subtle parallax across the existing paper-preview layers.
- Workflow: reveal the six actual workflow steps progressively.
- Capabilities: stagger the screening, collaboration, and extraction preview.
- Peak: pin the evidence scene while separate record fragments connect along a traceable path.
- Methodology: reveal the reporting pathway and PRISMA 2020 support without implying endorsement.
- Close: return to natural flow for the final CTA.

## Authored silence
After the separate records have connected in the pinned evidence scene, hold the completed path briefly before the methodology scene. This still interval is intentional; the diagram is complete and readable at this point.

## Visual contract
Use the existing white and blue design tokens, current font, real logo, semantic research UI, thin rules, and restrained shadows. Keep copy visible without JavaScript. Respect reduced motion. External photos, videos, invented statistics, testimonials, and endorsements are out of scope.
