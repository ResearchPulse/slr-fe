# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Researchers conducting systematic literature reviews, project coordinators managing review teams, and administrators supporting the platform. They use the system to organize review work across the full PRISMA 2020 workflow.

## Product Purpose

The Systematic Review Support System provides a structured workspace for planning, conducting, and reporting systematic literature reviews. It helps teams keep review decisions and progress clear, traceable, and reproducible.

## Brand Personality

Professional, modern, and trustworthy. The product should feel clear, composed, and dependable while supporting careful academic work.

## Anti-references

- Ornate decoration and visual noise that distract from research tasks.
- Generic dashboard patterns that obscure the review workflow.
- Invented metrics, statuses, progress, or ownership that are not supported by actual data.

## Design Principles

- Make each review phase and its next action easy to understand.
- Prioritize the legibility and context of research evidence and decisions.
- Keep workflow states, roles, and displayed data accurate and traceable.
- Use purposeful, restrained interface elements that support the task.
- Make interactions accessible and usable across the review team.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA. Respect reduced-motion preferences and do not rely on color alone to communicate state.
